# Marketing Filters

UX case study by [Ravi Myneni](https://suvarnamyneni.github.io), Senior Product / UX Designer (AI-Native UX Designer at eXp Realty, Hyderabad).

**Live site:** https://suvarnamyneni.github.io/marketing-filters-case-study/

The work is a redesign of the product filters in IgniteRE Marketing (Spotlight), the catalog eXp Realty agents use to order marketing materials. The project goal, from the source document: streamline filtering with an intuitive interface, clear categorization, and smart search, so agents spend less time searching and feel more confident picking the right asset. Tools named in the document: Figma and Miro. Research used Pendo and a UserZoom survey.

## What the research showed

Pendo usage (click charts are undated; the device chart is January 24, 2022 – January 24, 2023):

- Filter categories by clicks: Theme 12,260, Size 8,948, Holiday 3,672, Number of Photos 1,659. The slide says Number of Photos was used significantly less than the others and prints that comparison as p>0.05.
- Most-used individual filters include Size — Jumbo (3,225), Theme — Property marketing (3,207), and Theme — Seller directed (2,543). EDDM is the only top-10 filter outside Size and Theme (954).
- Least-used filters are mostly photo counts (14 photos has 17 clicks). Size — Tag has 21. Tags on the homepage has 15, despite a prominent homepage placement.
- Homepage categories: Postcard 12,227, Flyer 4,314, Brochure 3,029. All Products was the most-clicked feature in Marketing at 12,530. The document reads that as a need for better filtering.
- Postcards and Flyers lead the homepage, but the filters called those materials “small” and “8.5 × 11.”

Eleven people answered a UserZoom survey with an open card sort of 30 materials (an example card is “Welcome Home Tag”) and nine questions on frequency, navigation, pain points, and ease. Respondents: 6 sales reps, 3 sales managers, 1 product analyst, 1 real estate agent, in AZ (3), TX (2), FL (2), OH, HI, NM, and NV. The intended audience was agents and the sales reps who order for them.

- Card sort: 6 grouped by size, 3 by a mix of size and theme, 2 by theme.
- Average ease of finding materials with the filters in IRE Marketing or faPrint: **5.73** on a scale of 1 (difficult) to 7 (easy). The chart shows five ratings at 7, three at 6, one each at 5, 4, and 1.
- Devices: the survey shows 11 desktop/laptop, 1 mobile, 0 tablet. Pendo for that year shows 25,731 desktop, 1,802 mobile, 90 tablet.

## The solution in the document

People described **OR logic within a category** and **AND logic between categories**. The final slide annotates the existing columns rather than presenting a separate mock:

- Size is regrouped into Postcards (Small, Jumbo), Flyers (8.5 × 11), Brochures, and Booklets.
- Football, Baseball, and Hockey are grouped as Sports. Calendars is called out on its own.
- The Holiday heading is replaced with Season. Long photo-count lists sit behind See more. EDDM stays a single option.
- The catalog screenshot in the deck already shows product-type labels (Postcards Small, Brochures Trifold, Flyers 8.5×11), a result total of 252, per-option counts, search, Clear, and See less. The document does not tie that screen to a launch metric.

## What is deliberately not claimed

The source document has no timeline, no named team, no wireframe frames, no usability-test results, and no before/after impact. The live page marks those gaps as design rationale or as ways success could be measured against the baselines above. Participant quotes are not invented: the Miro canvases are in the deck, and the note text is not legible.
